Contact us

We are a small team, so you will hear from a real person. Pick the lane that fits and we will get back to you within two business days.

Technical support

Something not working?

The help centre is usually the fastest route. It covers logging, streaks, targets, account and billing. If your answer is not there, send a support request and include your device and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Account, data and refunds

Export or delete your data, fix a login problem, or ask about a charge. Write to contact@gymbroweb.com from the email on your account so we can find you.

Press and partnerships

Writing about GymBro, or want to work with us? Gyms, coaches, creators and brands are all welcome. Email contact@gymbroweb.com and tell us what you have in mind.

Feedback and ideas

Got a feature request, a complaint, or a streak you are proud of? We read all of it and it shapes what we build next. Send feedback.

Careers

Internship roles are posted on our careers page. If nothing is listed yet, send what you have built to contact@gymbroweb.com and we will keep you in mind.
